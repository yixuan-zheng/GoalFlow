# Harbor Home design — revision 1

The hero headline is “Find your next step.” Supporting copy describes short, friendly evening workshops. A workshop list shows subject, date and accessibility information. The primary CTA says “Register interest”; it scrolls to an embedded form rather than promising a confirmed place.

The interest form currently asks for name and email, followed by an unchecked consent box. It appears in a two-column panel on desktop and stacks on mobile. Current draft annotations ask reviewers to decide whether the form needs a longer introductory explanation, a multi-step presentation, or a compact single-step presentation. Any new fields need scope approval; layout alternatives do not imply permission to collect additional data.

The workshop cards use a blue heading on a pale background. Reviewers should check readability and the focus state on the CTA. Learner testimonials appear below the cards; no participant photographs or identifiable testimonials are included in the synthetic draft. The footer links About, Contact and the privacy notice.

Open review questions are the preferred form layout, the heading's clarity for adult learners, and whether the workshop cards need more contrast. Feedback must cite revision 1. A preference from a reviewer is not an approval to implement an incompatible change. Once a layout decision is made, Tomas needs explicit acceptance conditions for mobile and keyboard interaction.
