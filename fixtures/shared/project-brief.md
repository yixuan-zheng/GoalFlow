# Harbor Learning — HL-website refresh

## Objective and audience

Harbor Learning provides evening workshops for adults returning to education. Visitors include first-time learners, people using a mobile phone with a slow connection, and returning participants who want to find the next available session. The refresh must explain the programme clearly and collect expressions of interest. A conversion is a completed interest form, not a paid booking.

## Deliverables

Cedar Studio will refresh Home, Workshops, About and Contact. The first design review covers the Home page and its interest form. The implementation team needs one coherent revision brief rather than a collection of forwarded email messages. The brief must identify the current design revision, list each required reviewer's contribution, link original evidence, distinguish decisions from suggestions, and assign accepted changes to Tomas with observable acceptance conditions.

## Non-negotiable acceptance conditions

A signup/interest form remains present. Its approved fields are name and email unless the scope owner authorizes a change. Consent is explicit; the design must not preselect marketing consent. The primary CTA must describe interest registration rather than a guaranteed seat. Text contrast and keyboard navigation are required, and no review may silently waive accessibility acceptance. Layout, phrasing and visual emphasis are subject to the named client approver. Changing the mandatory signup requirement or collecting additional sensitive personal information requires Mira's scope decision.

## Ownership

Mira owns scope, sequencing and the customer handoff. Leo reviews programme clarity and Nia reviews learner accessibility and mobile usability. Both are required reviewers.
