# Cedar Studio — company handbook

Cedar Studio is a fictional six-person digital design agency. It builds small public-facing websites for education organizations. All names, companies, emails, commercial terms and project content in this lab are synthetic.

Mira Chen leads client delivery and is accountable for schedule and scope. Tomas Reed implements approved designs. The local coordinator acts on Mira's delegation but cannot approve design disputes or change the contract. A task status is a record of work, not proof of acceptance.

Project Harbor Learning is a four-page website refresh. Harbor offers evening adult-learning workshops. The site must help visitors find a workshop and register interest without promising that registration guarantees a place. Harbor contacts Leo and Nia review the design; Ada is the client approver. Bea is an alternate approver only when Mira explicitly assigns her that role.

Records live in separate document, message and task stores. A review message must identify the deliverable version. Publication means an approved internal revision brief is available to the delivery team; client-facing summaries must omit internal margins. This miniature world never sends real email or accesses real client records.
